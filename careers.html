Care for people. Be cared for too.

We are a small, women-owned practice looking for registered nurses and support workers across Melbourne and Geelong. Consistent rosters, honest hours, and you are never sent somewhere without a brief.

Who we are looking for

AHPRA-registered nurses with community experience, and support workers with NDIS Worker Screening who want steady work rather than scattered shifts. Experience with continence care, catheter management, wound care or high-intensity supports is valuable, but we will train the right person.

What we offer

Rosters built for continuity, so you work with the same participants rather than a different house each day. Travel time between clients is paid. Clinical questions go to a registered nurse, not a call centre. And if a placement is not working for you, you can say so.

We are a small practice, which has trade-offs worth being honest about. We cannot offer the shift volume of a large agency, and we do not have a big back office. What we can offer is that the person rostering you knows your name and has done the job themselves.

Requirements

  • AHPRA registration for nursing roles
  • NDIS Worker Screening Check
  • Working With Children Check where applicable
  • Australian drivers licence and reliable vehicle
  • Right to work in Australia
