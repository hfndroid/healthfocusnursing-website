Coordinator and plan manager questions

Can not find what you need? Call us on 0405 468 427.

Coordinators and plan managers can refer by phone on 0405 468 427, by email to info@healthfocusnursing.com.au, or using the referral form on this page. Health Focus Nursing acknowledges every referral within one business day and issues a service agreement within 48 hours.

To discuss a participant before referring, call us on 0405 468 427.

Health Focus Nursing contacts the participant or their representative within one business day of receiving a referral. There is currently no waitlist, so an initial home visit is typically arranged shortly afterwards rather than queued.

For an urgent referral, call us directly on 0405 468 427.

A referral needs the participant's first name and suburb, the service required, the plan type, and your contact details. Clinical detail is helpful but not essential at referral stage, since a registered nurse completes a full assessment at the first home visit.

If you are unsure what to include, call us on 0405 468 427.

Health Focus Nursing is registered across eight NDIS support classes: 0104, 0107, 0108, 0114, 0116, 0117, 0120 and 0125. This includes community nursing care for high needs and high-intensity daily personal activities such as complex wound management, catheter management, enteral feeding, tracheostomy and ventilator management, and severe dysphagia management.

To confirm registration for a specific support, call us on 0405 468 427.
