What disability support actually looks like

Most people do not need everything. They need help with two or three specific parts of the week that have become hard to manage alone, and they want the person helping to be someone they know.

That is how Health Focus Nursing builds support. We start with what you are finding difficult, match you with workers whose approach suits you, and keep familiar faces coming back. If something is not quite right, tell us and we will adjust it together. Where a clinical task is involved, a registered nurse looks after it.

How we work

  • We ask what you want help with before proposing anything
  • You are always welcome to ask for a different worker
  • We keep familiar faces coming back wherever we can
  • If anything changes with a visit, we call you first
  • When funding or health changes, we adjust the roster
  • Clinical tasks go to a registered nurse, not a support worker

Who we support

We support NDIS participants of all ages and support levels, from people who need occasional help getting out into the community through to those with high support needs requiring coordinated clinical and personal care. We also work with families needing respite, and with coordinators managing complex arrangements.

How it is funded

Disability support sits under Core Supports, covering Assistance with Daily Life and Assistance with Social and Community Participation. Where needs are more complex, high-intensity daily personal activities are delivered under registered nurse supervision. All services are billed at current NDIS Price Guide rates across self-managed, plan-managed and NDIA-managed plans.
