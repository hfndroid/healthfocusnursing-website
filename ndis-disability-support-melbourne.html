Person-centred approach

Person centred support means the plan is built around your goals, routines and preferences rather than a fixed template. Before support begins, we take the time to understand what matters to you, whether that is staying connected to your community, maintaining independence at home, or building specific skills. We then match you with support workers whose approach and personality genuinely suit you, and we aim to keep the same workers involved over time so you are not explaining your needs to someone new every visit.

Who we support

We support NDIS participants of all ages and support needs, from people who need occasional help with community access through to those with high support needs requiring coordinated clinical and personal care. We also work closely with families and carers who need respite, and with support coordinators managing complex care arrangements.

How it is funded

Disability support services are typically funded under Core Supports, covering categories such as Assistance with Daily Life and Assistance with Social and Community Participation. Where a participant has more complex needs, high intensity daily personal activities can be funded and delivered under registered nurse supervision. All services are billed at current NDIS Price Guide rates across self managed, plan managed and NDIA managed plans.
