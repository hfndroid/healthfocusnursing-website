Our services

Most people come to us for one of these three. Many end up combining two.

NDIS Continence Assessment

Capacity Building

A registered nurse visits you at home, talks through what is happening and writes the formal report your NDIS planner needs.

  • Home visit, usually 45 to 90 minutes
  • Formal written report for your planner
  • Product and equipment recommendations
Read more ›

Community Nursing

NDIS 0114

Clinical care at home by AHPRA-registered nurses, from routine dressings through to high-intensity supports.

  • Catheter, wound and medication management
  • Bowel and stoma care, diabetes support
  • Tracheostomy, ventilator and enteral feeding
Read more ›

Disability Support

NDIS 0107, 0120, 0125

Help with the parts of the week that are hardest alone, with familiar faces coming back rather than a rotating roster.

  • Personal care and daily living support
  • Domestic assistance and meal preparation
  • Community access, dementia support and respite
Read more ›

Not sure which you need?

That is the most common place people start, and it is not something you need to resolve before calling. Tell us what is difficult at the moment and we will work out which supports fit, and whether your plan has the funding for them.
