Four things people usually call us about

These are the services we get asked about most. The full list, with NDIS item numbers, is below and on each service page.

NDIS Continence Assessment

A registered nurse comes to you, talks through what is actually going on, and writes the formal report your planner needs. Reports are written clearly, so your planner has what they need.

Community Nursing

High Intensity Supports

Disability Support

More ways we can help

Domestic assistance, social and community participation, companion care, dementia support, palliative care, in-home respite, diabetes management, overnight nursing and hospital-to-home transition care are all part of what we offer too.

Domestic Assistance Social and Community Participation Companion Care Diabetes Management Overnight Nursing Transition Care Program Bowel and Stoma Care

Continence assessments are funded under Capacity Building, Improved Daily Living. Nursing services sit under NDIS 0114. Personal care and dementia support sit under 0107, domestic assistance under 0120, and community participation under 0125.

We also hold NDIS registration for high-intensity daily personal activities, including complex wound management, urinary catheter management, complex bowel care, enteral feeding, tracheostomy and ventilator management, subcutaneous injections and severe dysphagia management. Registered support classes: 0104, 0107, 0108, 0114, 0116, 0117, 0120, 0125.

Your care, our focus

Health Focus Nursing was started by a registered nurse who has spent years alongside patients and the families who love them, first in oncology and mental health placements, then in community care right through Melbourne's lockdowns. We are a small, women-owned practice, and everyone is welcome here, including our LGBTQ+ community.

What you can expect from us:

  • Your choiceYou are always welcome to ask for a different support worker. We will simply find a better fit.
  • Familiar facesWe keep the same people coming back where we can, so your routines and preferences are already understood.
  • Reliable visitsWe keep the times we agree to, and if anything changes, we call you first.
  • Room to changeAs your needs or funding change, we adjust your support with you.

Community nursing covers clinical tasks that must be performed or supervised by a registered nurse, such as wound care, catheter management, medication administration and enteral feeding, and is funded under NDIS item 0114. Personal care covers non-clinical help with daily living, such as showering, dressing, grooming and meal preparation, is delivered by support workers, and is funded under NDIS item 0107. Many participants receive both.

If you are unsure which your plan funds, call us on 0405 468 427.
