Not sure which support fits your plan?

Most people searching for an NDIS provider are dealing with one specific need, not a catalogue. Below is the full range so you can find yours, from personal care and domestic assistance through to complex clinical nursing and continence assessments, delivered across Melbourne, Geelong and surrounding areas.

Personal and Self-care

Assistance with daily personal activities including hygiene, grooming and self-care for NDIS participants. Delivered by experienced support workers across Melbourne and Geelong.

About us

Your care, our focus

At Health Focus Nursing, we are dedicated to providing care and support services that make a real difference in people's lives. Founded by an experienced registered nurse, our mission is to empower individuals with disabilities and complex health needs to live life to the fullest.

We are an NDIS-registered provider serving Melbourne, Geelong and surrounding areas with a team of experienced registered nurses and dedicated support workers who are passionate about person-centred care. Women-owned and LGBTQ+ friendly, we believe everyone deserves care that respects their identity, their choices and their goals.

Respect

We respect the dignity and autonomy of every individual we support.

Compassion

We provide care with kindness, empathy and genuine understanding.

Excellence

We strive for excellence in every service and every interaction.

How it works

Three steps to getting started

Accessing our services is straightforward. We handle the complexity so you can focus on what matters.

1

Enquire or refer

Submit the form, call us directly, or ask your support coordinator to send a referral. We respond within one business day.

2

Assessment and planning

We visit you at home, assess your needs and build a personalised care plan aligned to your NDIS goals and funding.

3

Your care begins

Services commence on your agreed schedule. We manage all billing directly through your NDIS plan.
