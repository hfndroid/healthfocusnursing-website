Four things people usually call us about

These are the services we get asked about most. The full list, with NDIS item numbers, is below and on each service page.

NDIS Continence Assessment

A registered nurse comes to you, talks through what is actually going on, and writes the formal report your planner needs. Most of our reports are accepted the first time they are submitted.

Community Nursing

Catheter care, wound management, medication management, bowel and stoma care, right through to tracheostomy and ventilator support. Delivered by AHPRA-registered nurses, not general support workers.

Personal Care and Daily Support

Help with the parts of the day that are hardest to manage alone. hygiene, grooming, meal prep, getting to appointments and out into the community. We try to keep the same faces coming back.

Everything else we do

Domestic assistance, social and community participation, companion care, diabetes management, overnight nursing and hospital-to-home transition care are all part of what we offer too.

Domestic Assistance Social and Community Participation Companion Care Diabetes Management Overnight Nursing Transition Care Program Bowel and Stoma Care

Continence assessments are funded under Capacity Building, Improved Daily Living. Nursing services sit under NDIS 0114. Personal care sits under 0107, dementia support under 0104, domestic assistance under 0120, and community participation under 0125.

We also hold NDIS registration for high-intensity daily personal activities, including complex wound management, catheter management, enteral feeding, tracheostomy and ventilator management, subcutaneous injections and severe dysphagia management. Registered support classes: 0104, 0107, 0108, 0114, 0116, 0117, 0120, 0125.

Your care, our focus

Health Focus Nursing is a small, women-owned practice run by a registered nurse. We are LGBTQ+ friendly because our participants are, not because it reads well on a website.

What that means in practice:

  • You chooseIf you want a different support worker, say so. We will change it without making it awkward.
  • Same facesWe roster for continuity, so you are not re-explaining your catheter routine to a stranger every fortnight.
  • We turn upIf we say Tuesday at ten, we are there Tuesday at ten. If we are running late, you get a call, not silence.
  • Plans changeWhen your funding or your health shifts, we adjust the roster rather than telling you to wait for a review.
