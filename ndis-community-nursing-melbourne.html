What is NDIS community nursing?

NDIS community nursing brings clinical nursing care into the home instead of requiring participants to travel to a clinic or hospital. It covers a wide range of tasks that must be performed or supervised by a registered nurse, from routine wound dressings through to complex, high intensity clinical support.

At Health Focus Nursing, every community nursing visit is carried out by an AHPRA-registered nurse, so participants with complex or fluctuating health needs receive care that is both clinically safe and properly documented. This matters for continuity of care between visits, and it matters for NDIS reporting and funding reviews, where clear clinical notes make a real difference.

Who we support

Our community nursing team supports participants with complex health needs, people transitioning home after a hospital stay, and those managing chronic conditions that require regular clinical monitoring. We also provide the high intensity supports needed by participants with tracheostomies, ventilators, enteral feeding tubes or severe dysphagia, working closely with treating teams to keep care consistent and safe.

How it is funded

Community nursing care is funded under NDIS support item 0114 and billed at current NDIS Price Guide rates. It is accessible across self managed, plan managed and NDIA managed plans, and can be combined with high intensity daily personal activity funding where more complex clinical supports are required.
