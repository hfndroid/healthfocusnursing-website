What is NDIS community nursing?

Community nursing means the clinical care you would normally travel to a clinic or hospital for happens at home instead. For participants managing catheters, wounds, complex medication routines or high-intensity needs, that removes a lot of difficulty from an ordinary week.

Every visit at Health Focus Nursing is carried out by an AHPRA-registered nurse, not a general support worker. That distinction matters twice over. Clinically, because complex needs require someone trained to notice when something is changing. Administratively, because clear clinical notes are what hold up at plan review time.

High-intensity supports

Some clinical needs require advanced training and registered nurse oversight. Health Focus Nursing holds NDIS registration to deliver these as high-intensity daily personal activities.

  • Complex wound management
  • Urinary catheter management, in-dwelling, in-out and suprapubic
  • Complex bowel care
  • Enteral feeding via naso-gastric, jejunum and duodenum routes
  • Tracheostomy and ventilator management
  • Subcutaneous injections and severe dysphagia management

Who we support

Community nursing suits participants with complex or changing health needs, people coming home after a hospital stay, and those managing chronic conditions that need regular clinical review. We also support participants with tracheostomies, ventilators, feeding tubes or severe dysphagia, working alongside treating teams so care stays consistent.

How it is funded

Community nursing is funded under NDIS support item 0114 and billed at current NDIS Price Guide rates. It works across self-managed, plan-managed and NDIA-managed plans, and can sit alongside high-intensity daily personal activity funding where more complex clinical support is required.

Community nursing covers clinical tasks that must be performed or supervised by a registered nurse, such as wound care, catheter management and medication administration, funded under NDIS item 0114. Personal care covers non-clinical help with daily living such as showering, dressing and grooming, delivered by support workers under NDIS item 0107. Many participants receive both.

If you are unsure which your plan funds, call us on 0405 468 427.
