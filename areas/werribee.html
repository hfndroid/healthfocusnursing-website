NDIS support in Werribee

Health Focus Nursing visits participants across Werribee, postcode 3030, in the City of Wyndham. We are a home visit service, which means a registered nurse or support worker comes to you rather than asking you to travel to a clinic.

Wyndham is one of the fastest growing council areas in the country, and Werribee sits at the centre of it. Many of the participants we support here are in newer estates where services have not caught up with the population, and where finding a provider who will actually travel out is the first hurdle.

Distance is the practical issue in Werribee. Some providers quote for the area and then struggle to staff it consistently. We would rather confirm a schedule we can hold than take on a roster we cannot cover, so we are direct about availability when you call.

What we deliver

The service we are asked for most in Werribee is the nurse-led continence assessment. A registered nurse visits, reviews medical and continence history, assesses the functional impact on daily life, and writes the formal report your NDIS planner needs. Reports are written clearly for an NDIS audience, so your planner has what they need.

Alongside that we provide community nursing under NDIS item 0114, covering wound care, catheter management, medication administration, bowel and stoma care and diabetes support. For participants with more complex needs we are registered to deliver high-intensity daily personal activities, including complex wound management, enteral feeding, tracheostomy and ventilator management and severe dysphagia management.

On the support side we provide personal care under item 0107, domestic assistance under 0120, and social and community participation under 0125. Dementia support sits under item 0104, and we also provide palliative care at home and in-home respite for family carers. Participants in Werribee frequently combine two or three of these rather than taking everything.

Funding and plan types

All services are billed at current NDIS Price Guide rates and are available to self-managed, plan-managed and NDIA-managed participants in Werribee. Continence assessments are funded under Capacity Building, Improved Daily Living. Nursing sits under Core Supports at item 0114. For eligible participants with the relevant funding already in their plan, there is no out-of-pocket cost.

If you are not certain which category your funding sits in, that is normal and it is not something you need to resolve before calling. We check it with you, and if the funding is not there we say so rather than starting a service that cannot be billed.
